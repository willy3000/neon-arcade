# WILDFALL — Asset manifest

Everything shipped in `wildfall/assets/` and `wildfall/vendor/` is listed here with its source and licence. Every third-party asset is CC0 or MIT; no attribution is legally required, but credit is given in the game's settings panel and below. Licence texts were read from the downloaded packs on 2026-10-09 before anything was integrated.

`wildfall/assets/` is generated by `node wildfall/tools/prepare-assets.mjs <downloads-dir>` from the original packs. The packs themselves are not committed. `wildfall/assets/integrity.json` records the size and SHA-256 of every generated file.

## What was changed

- **Animation trimming.** Each KayKit character file carried every clip (3.5–4.8 MB each, 29 MB for the seven characters). The tool keeps only the clips the game plays and drops tracks that never leave the rest pose.
- **Shared clip libraries.** The tool checks that the hero rigs are identical (maximum rest-pose difference 1.2e-7) and that the four skeleton rigs are identical, then writes one animation file per family (`hero-anims.glb`, `skeleton-anims.glb`) and mesh-only files for each character. Total after processing: about 9 MB for everything in `assets/`.
- **Meshes and textures are unmodified.** Tints (the Sky Wraith and Emberhusk) and scale are applied at runtime.
- **Sounds are unmodified** apart from renaming. Pitch and volume vary per hero at runtime.

## Made for this game (no third-party source)

- Island, platform, wall, gate, ice and bloom geometry — built at load time from level data so the visuals always match the collision.
- Sky gradient, waterfalls, particles, shock rings, lightning, rope, weapon trails, cloth strips — shaders and procedural meshes in `render/`.
- Stone, wood and crack textures — drawn to canvas at load time in `render/assets.js`.
- Wind and drone ambience — synthesised with WebAudio in `audio.js`. There is no composed music.
- `cover.jpg` — a screenshot of the game.

## Evaluated but not used

Quaternius, Mixamo, OpenGameArt and itch.io packs were considered. KayKit was chosen because one creator's packs supply heroes, enemies, ruins and nature in a single style on a single rig, which removed retargeting and style-mixing problems. No assets from the other sources were downloaded or integrated.

## Known gaps

- No flying-creature model exists in these packs, so the Sky Wraith is a scaled, tinted Skeleton Mage playing its airborne clips.
- The packs have no hanging, swinging, wall-running or sliding clips. Those poses are built from existing clips plus procedural lean, roll and an arm that aims at the grapple anchor.
- The Gatewarden is a scaled Skeleton Warrior rather than a bespoke boss model.
