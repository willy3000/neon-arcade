A FIELD GUIDE TO MAKING AN ENTRANCE

MOVE FAST. HIT HARD.

01

THE TWO WARRIORS

Blaze swings a heavy sword and ignites the battlefield. Volt uses quick blades and chain lightning. Solo gives you an AI partner. Press X to switch; both heroes stay in the fight.

02

COMBAT WITH INTENT

Tap attack to strike. Time another tap near the end to continue Blaze's three-hit or Volt's four-hit combo. Heavy strikes break reinforced crates and guard armor. Specials cost 35 energy. Ultimate needs a full charge.

Double-jump, wall-jump, and dash to reposition. Dash immunity starts just after launch and lasts briefly. Heavy attacks in the air slam downward. You can dash out of light or heavy recovery.

03

BREAK THE DISTANCE

Grapple attaches to a glowing anchor or nearby enemy. Steer to swing, then press again to release with momentum. Fast swings can kick enemies. Jump over red strips when their warning line fills.

Read enemy warning shapes. Gold outlines mark upcoming danger; red marks the active attack. Boss cores take more damage during recovery.

04

BETTER TOGETHER

Alternate hits to earn team bonuses. Volt electrifies Blaze's fire zones. When fusion is full, both players confirm within two seconds while close. In solo, your AI confirms for you.

Stand within reach of a downed partner for 1.45 seconds without attacking to revive them. Rescue within 20 seconds. Both down means a checkpoint retry.

Clear each encounter, break the generator, then bring both heroes to extraction. Collect gold relics for another star. A fast run with ≤100 team damage and no falls earns the third.
