# BLOOD OATH — Asset manifest

Everything shipped in `bloodoath/` is listed here with its source and licence. All third-party material is CC0 (public domain dedication): no attribution is legally required, but credit is given in the game's Settings panel and below. Nothing in the game is taken from Mortal Kombat or any other commercial fighting game: no characters, names, moves, sounds, music, logos or screen layouts.

`bloodoath/assets/` is generated by `node bloodoath/tools/prepare-assets.mjs <downloads-dir>` from the original downloads, which are not committed. `bloodoath/assets/integrity.json` records the size and SHA-256 of all 48 generated files (13.85 MiB: 45 sound effects, 3 music tracks).

## Made for this game

No third-party art is used. Research before building found no CC0 sprite pack that covers several distinct fighters with a full fighting-game move list (the candidates had an idle, a run, a jump and two or three attacks), so every character is drawn by the game itself.

- **Fighters (Cinder, Rime, Vesper, Grit, Malgrave).** Each is a cut-out figure drawn in code over a shared skeleton (`render/rig.js`, `render/skins.js`): limbs are placed by two-bone inverse kinematics from hand-authored key poses in `data/*.js`, with cloth strips simulated for scarves, coats and capes. Names, designs, move names and silhouettes are original.
- **Arenas (Ashen Temple, Hollow Yard, Frozen Shrine, Oath Pit).** Painted in layers to off-screen canvases at load time, with parallax and live elements (embers, mist, snow, a watching eye) in `render/arenas.js`.
- **Effects.** Hit sparks, blood, embers, ice shards, dust, shock rings, projectiles, afterimages and the ward are particles and shapes in `render/view.js`.
- **Arena ambience.** Fire, wind, blizzard and a low drone are synthesised from filtered noise and oscillators in `audio.js`; there are no ambience files.
- **Interface.** HTML and CSS in `blood-oath.html` and `style.css`. The library cover (`cover.jpg`) is a frame captured from the game.
