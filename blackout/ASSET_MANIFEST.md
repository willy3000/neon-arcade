# Asset sources and redistribution

All listed assets were actually downloaded and prepared locally. Runtime assets live under `assets/`; original ZIPs and extracted source files are ignored under `assets/source/`. `tools/prepare_assets.py` rebuilds the atlases with Pillow. No copyrighted game characters or commercial game artwork are included.

Required Riley attribution: **Animated Top Down Survivor Player by Riley Gombart, licensed CC BY 3.0. Modified palette, size and atlas layout.** The settings credit links directly to the creator's source; source page links the license. The manifest supplies both direct links and changes for redistribution compliance.

Research: the initial `kenney.nl/assets/topdown-shooter` URL and the `foley-sounds` URL returned 404. The correct official shooter URL is `top-down-shooter`; Impact Sounds supplied compatible movement/impact samples. OpenGameArt's CC0 static soldier SVG and a basic military idle/walk pack on itch.io were considered but did not cover gun/reload/melee/feet sequences. Riley's animation-ready pack was selected after inspecting actual frame sizes and a contact sheet. Gunshots by kurt were considered but not used because recording provenance was less explicit than the creator-authored Residue pack. Craftpix, Freesound and Quaternius assets were not acquired; their redistribution terms are not assumed or claimed.

No paid asset, image generator or asset purchase was needed. Future animation additions must retain these credits or use separately verified sources.

Interface tones, the low-health heartbeat and breathing, bullet whiz, flashbang ringing and the per-theme drone score are synthesized at runtime with Web Audio; they are not sampled assets.

`docs/ASSET_LICENSE_REGISTER.md` (13 October 2026) goes through everything that ships, separates licences confirmed at the source from those only recorded here, and lists what must be settled before a commercial release: the commander voice and the lines generated from it. The menu music was confirmed on 14 October 2026.

`assets/integrity.json` records the file sizes and SHA-256 hashes of all redistributable game assets.

The Top-down Shooter license (`assets/licenses/kenney-props.txt`) uses LF line endings, enforced by `.gitattributes` and the asset preparation script. Its integrity entry reflects that representation so Git checkout line-ending conversion does not invalidate the checksum. The license text is unchanged.
