# Operation Blackout — campaign

Story: Rook's helicopter is shot down over occupied Vesper City and his extraction is destroyed by a missile from his own side. A stranger on the radio, **Lark**, a signals analyst inside his command, helps him trace the order to **Colonel Marek Voss (CONTROL ACTUAL)**, who uses the private army **Halcyon** and the "Blackout Protocol" kill list to erase witnesses.

## Playable — Acts I and II

| # | Operation | Landscape | Problem it poses | Recommended | Finale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | THE CRASH | City streets | Learn cover, slides, vaults; clear zones | — | Hold the courtyard, commander, betrayal missile |
| 2 | NO SAFE GROUND | Ruined Old Quarter at dusk | Three jammers in any order across close-quarters ruins; weak walls as shortcuts | Shotgun + frags | 55s defence of the metro shutter |
| 3 | DEAD FREQUENCY | Night snowfield + relay compound | Cross open ground under fence marksmen; frozen lake splits the approach | Marksman/precision rifle + smoke | 70s uplink defence, then the **Wraith** sniper duel |
| 4 | UNDERGROUND | Blacked-out Line 6 tunnels | Fight inside your flashlight; ghosts and rushers; restore power in stages | Shotgun + flashbangs | 60s platform hold for the train |
| 5 | IRON CONVOY | Desert highway | Burn two fuel tankers, clear the halted column across open ground | Explosives (M79 / AT4 / mines) | **Bulwark** armoured carrier |
| 6 | GHOST SIGNAL | Halcyon black-site labs | Front-armoured sentries, cycling laser grids, server racks behind glass, a vent bypass | EMP + pulse/rail | **Warden** mech fed by shield generators |

Checkpoints save at each stage, after every destroyed objective target, and remember which soldiers are already dead. The final operation ends on a cliffhanger: Voss traces Lark.
