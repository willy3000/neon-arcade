# Operation Blackout

## Playable: Mission 1 — THE CRASH

Rook survives the loss of ROOK-07. A distressed radio call gives a clear immediate objective and action begins at the crash site. Three patrol opponents guard the relay. A voice on the recovered channel knows he should be dead, but points him toward a supply cache.

The alley encounter introduces flanking infantry, a charging attacker and a sniper. The cache grants the M870 and three frags in-world, replenishes practical supplies and saves a checkpoint. A damaged-window route and low barriers offer optional traversal shortcuts.

The extraction courtyard combines infantry, a shield, a rusher and a sniper across parking bays. Activating the beacon saves the fourth checkpoint. Two warned reinforcement groups arrive; the commander changes attack pattern at two health thresholds. The director grants limited recovery/resource support rather than secretly inflating enemy health.

After the commander dies, the beacon becomes available. The extraction transmission is interrupted by a friendly missile. Letterboxing, a nearby explosion, radio dialogue and the final report reveal that the crash was a deliberate execution. THE CRASH ends here; the next chapter is named as development work. No fake continue button launches an unfinished mission.

Checkpoints: crash (stage 0), recovered relay (1), supply cache (2), activated extraction beacon (3). Restarting a checkpoint reconstructs its encounter and grants safe health/equipment at an authored position. Stage 4 restores stage 3 so closing during the last seconds cannot strand the player after the boss.
