Two matching exits. A portal keeps its spark safe, but both must arrive before a room is complete.

Read the dimensions. Amber terrain affects Kai. Cyan terrain affects Luma. Striped purple veils block Kai and let Luma through. Neutral stone supports both.

Link at purple nodes. Bring both close, then interact. An active tether powers shared gates and limits separation. Interact away from other mechanisms to unlink.

Solo and mobile. Use either set of movement keys or the touch buttons. Press X or tap SWITCH to select a character. Park the other on a plate or ferry before switching.

Stay responsive. Hold jump for height; tap for a short hop. R restarts; Escape pauses. Hint reveals one of three clues. Flag checkpoints save both positions and puzzle mechanisms.
