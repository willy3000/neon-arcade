REMEMBER WHAT YOU CAN BECOME

A FIELD GUIDE.

01 / MOVEMENT

Make empty space feel alive.

A D or ← → move. Space / W jumps twice. Hold for height. Jump off walls; press toward a wall to slide. Shift dashes. Hold up or down to aim an air dash. S crouches, slides while moving, or fast-falls.

Q attaches or releases a gold temporal anchor. Hold toward it to shorten the tether; steer to swing. Dash within 0.16 seconds after release for Momentum Snap.

02 / COMBAT

Timing gives a blade meaning.

J or left click slashes. Tap near recovery to chain three hits. Hold K or right mouse for a charged heavy; release to strike. Heavy in the air slams down. W + J launches. Slash during a dash for a dash attack.

F parries briefly. L spends 30 special for Zero Hour, damaging and slowing enemies. U triggers Afterstorm at full ultimate. H heals once per run. 1 2 3 switch unlocked weapons outside attacks.

03 / ECHO SHIFT

Your past is a tactical partner.

E spends 35 echo energy to replay the last three seconds. The ice-blue echo follows your recorded path, but its strikes hit enemies where they are now. You remain free to act. Energy regenerates; cooldown is 4.5 seconds.

Strike from the other side for Crossfire. Connect almost simultaneously for Perfect Sync. Echo parries and dashes can intercept projectiles. C spends 20 special to collapse active echoes into nearby enemies.

At a relay, stand still for a moment, summon, then move. The replay can hold the door for you. Echo hunters can disrupt replays. Echoes phase through scenery and never copy your resource costs.

04 / THE RUN

Failure changes the next attempt.

Clear each sealed encounter. Choose one temporary mutation between scenes. Defeat the Clockwork Titan and reach the final portal. Repeated attacks work; varied attacks, parries and sync build style faster.

R instantly restarts. Escape pauses. Hidden tabs pause too. Standard gamepads: left stick moves; A jumps; X slashes; Y heavy; B dash; LB parry; RB echo; LT anchor; RT special; D-pad right collapse; D-pad down heal; right stick click ultimate; D-pad left switches weapons; Start pauses.

Touch solo offers movement, jump, attack, dash, heavy, echo, parry and an expandable ability row. Landscape gives more room. Assist reduces incoming damage; challenge records remain separate.
